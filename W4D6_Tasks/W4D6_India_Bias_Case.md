# India AI Bias Case: Facial Recognition

Facial recognition systems can perform unevenly across demographic groups when training data is not representative. In India, differences in skin tone, age, gender, and image conditions may affect accuracy. Incorrect matches can cause privacy violations, wrongful suspicion, or unnecessary police questioning. Overall accuracy alone cannot show whether a system treats groups fairly. Developers should evaluate false-positive and false-negative rates across relevant groups using representative Indian data. High-impact decisions should include human review, a meaningful appeal process, strict limits on data retention, and independent audits. Facial recognition should not be treated as conclusive evidence. Responsible AI requires transparency, accountability, privacy protection, and safeguards against disproportionate harm.
